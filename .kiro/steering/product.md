# Ghost from the Past — Product Vision

## Vision Statement

**"A ghost from your past that remembers every page you've read."**

A personal, privacy-first browser memory for knowledge workers, researchers, and curious readers. Ghost quietly captures and makes searchable every web article you read, storing it in your own Cloudflare account. No central server. No tracking. No data leaves your infrastructure.

## Users

1. **Knowledge workers** (engineers, researchers, writers): need to search across hundreds of articles they've read in the past month; tired of losing that one great explainer.
2. **AI enthusiasts using Claude, Kiro, or Cursor**: want to ask their AI assistant "what was that article about vector databases I read last week?" and get actual search results from their real reading history, not hallucinations.
3. **Privacy-conscious readers**: want the same ease as a read-it-later service but without handing their library to a third party.

## The Privacy Promise

- **No central server.** Everything runs in your Cloudflare Workers account.
- **No syncing to our infrastructure.** The extension talks only to your Worker. We never see the URLs or content.
- **Incognito is never captured.** Private browsing stays private.
- **You control redaction and blocklisting.** Strip e-mails, card numbers, and secret tokens by default. Block entire sites or patterns.
- **You can forget.** The `forget` tool in MCP can delete pages, domains, or date ranges.
- **You can delete everything.** Delete the Worker, the D1 database, and the Vectorize index. Ghosting is complete.

## Non-Goals

- **No central server.** We do not provide hosted infrastructure. Deployment is your responsibility (Cloudflare Workers is the intended platform; it's free-tier-friendly).
- **No Chrome Web Store publishing.** The extension is loaded unpacked by the user. This keeps us out of Google's review process and lets users audit the code.
- **No free tier beyond what Cloudflare offers.** We piggyback on Cloudflare's free tier limits (5M vector dimensions ≈ 13k chunks; D1 free quota). We don't cross-subsidise or provide a managed service.
- **No real-time sync.** Sync runs on a 5-minute timer or on-demand; latency is acceptable.
- **No AI inference on our servers.** Embeddings are generated by your Worker's Workers AI, which stays within your account. HashEmbedder fallback requires no external service.

## Market Positioning

Ghost is a "Frankenstein" entry for Kiroween 2026 — intentionally stitching together disparate pieces (browser extension, Cloudflare Worker, D1, Vectorize, Workers AI, MCP SDK) to show how to build a full-stack, privacy-respecting, AI-integrated application with no central authority.
